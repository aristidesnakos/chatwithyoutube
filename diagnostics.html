Manual Verification Steps

  1. Chrome Version: Must be 138+ (Dev/Canary)
  2. Flags: Visit chrome://flags/ and enable:
    • #prompt-api-for-gemini-nano
    • #optimization-guide-on-device-model
    • #experimental-extension-apis
    • #enable-unsafe-webgpu
    • #enable-experimental-web-platform-features
  3. Components: Visit chrome://components/ and look for "Optimization Guide On-Device Model"
  4. Hardware: Ensure 22GB storage and GPU >4GB VRAM
